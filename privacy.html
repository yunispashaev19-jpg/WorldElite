Privacy Policy

WorldElite
Last updated: August 15, 2026

WorldElite (“we”, “our”, “the app”) provides educational information about global wealth, companies, and financial literacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the WorldElite website or application.

1. Who we are

WorldElite is an informational product about billionaires, companies, and financial education. Contact: use the contact details listed on our GitHub profile or app store listing.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use WorldElite, the following may be processed:

  • Account data you enter locally (such as name or email if you use login/sign up). This may be stored on your device.
  • App preferences (for example Premium demo status) stored locally on your device.
  • Technical data such as device type, browser type, and approximate usage data if analytics or advertising SDKs are enabled in future versions.
  • Purchase data if you buy Premium or remove-ads through Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google, not by us directly.

3. How we use information

  • to operate and improve WorldElite;
  • to remember local preferences on your device;
  • to provide Premium features when available;
  • to show ads in free versions when advertising is enabled;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising

Current web version may not show third-party ads. Future Android versions may use Google AdMob or similar services. Those services may collect device identifiers and usage data according to their own policies.

5. Payments

If in-app purchases or subscriptions are offered, payments are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details.

6. Data storage

Some information is stored locally on your device using browser/app storage. We do not operate a guaranteed cloud account system in the current version.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by service providers such as Google (Play, Ads, analytics) only as needed to run the product.

8. Children’s privacy

WorldElite is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Financial information disclaimer

WorldElite provides educational and informational content only. It is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

10. Your choices

  • You can clear local app/site data in your browser or device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.
  • You can manage subscription billing through Google Play.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when revisions are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Google Play store listing or the project repository associated with WorldElite.
